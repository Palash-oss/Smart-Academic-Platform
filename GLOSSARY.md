# Smart Academic Platform

Academic operations for a college department: elective allotment, timetables, attendance, and student support.

## Language

### Timetable

**Timetable Slot**:
A recurring weekly entry in a division's timetable: a day of the week, a time range, and what is taught then.
_Avoid_: Period, class (for the time slot itself)

**Extra Lecture**:
A one-off lecture a faculty member adds on a specific calendar date, outside the recurring weekly timetable, for one division and one of their subjects.
_Avoid_: Extra class, makeup lecture, additional slot

### Organisation

**Division**:
A fixed group of students in a department and semester who attend theory lectures together (e.g. COMP-A, COMP-B).
_Avoid_: Class, form, section
